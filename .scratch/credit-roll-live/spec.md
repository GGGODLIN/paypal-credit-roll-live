## Problem Statement

Independent film, animation, podcast, and indie game productions treat their end-credits roll as a static thank-you screen while managing contributor contracts, stage-name mapping, cut-edit changes, fan support, and PayPal payouts in disconnected spreadsheets. When a director trims a scene between the rough cut (v1) and the director's cut (v2), a sound designer or VFX artist whose cue was cut often vanishes from the v2 credit roll even though their fixed contract fee remains approved and unpaid ("ghost credit"). Conversely, contributors credited under stage names or handles (such as `DJ Owl` or `Moth`) fail to match legal contract rosters, causing either missed payouts, duplicate payments, or accidental misdirected transfers.

## Solution

**CreditRoll Live** turns the rolling end-credits of a film or indie game inside **AG Studio** into an interactive, auditable financial map powered by AI reconciliation and real **PayPal Sandbox** money movement:

1. **Interactive Rolling Credit Roll & Ghost Diff Widgets**: Registered as native AG Studio Custom Widgets, the credit roll displays every contributor as a live financial node. Switching between Edit v1 (Rough Cut) and Edit v2 (Director's Cut) highlights removed contributors as glowing translucent **Ghost Credits** ("removed from cut ≠ contract fee waived") and cross-filters the companion AG Grid to show their cues, contracts, and PayPal settlement receipts.
2. **AI Stage-Name & Cue Sheet Reconciler with Deterministic Guardrails**: An AI domain agent reconciles unstructured v1/v2 credit text and cue sheets against the approved contract roster, resolving stage names to verified person IDs while forcing ambiguous or duplicate aliases into a human-review hold (`NEEDS_REVIEW`) and forbidding the model from inventing or altering preset contract amounts.
3. **Two-Way PayPal Sandbox Money Loop with Resource Read-Back**:
   - **Audience / Producer Funding (`Orders v2 CAPTURE` & `Invoicing v2 + QR`)**: Viewers or executive producers can purchase the Behind-the-Scenes (BTS) Digital Supporter Pack directly from the credit roll via PayPal Orders (`intent: CAPTURE`) or generate a PayPal Invoice with a scannable QR code for unpaid production obligations.
   - **Crew Batch Settlement (`Payouts v1` + Per-Item Verification)**: Once the producer approves the reconciled roster, the server executes an idempotent PayPal Sandbox batch payout (`POST /v1/payments/payouts`) and queries back batch and individual item statuses (`GET /v1/payments/payouts/{batch_id}`), stamping each credit roll node as `PAID` only when PayPal returns `SUCCESS` (while preserving `PENDING`, `UNCLAIMED`, or `FAILED` states honestly).

## Implementation Decisions

- **Relational Studio Data Model Extension**: Extend the existing dashboard data layer with structured relational tables for `crewMembers` (verified person ID, legal name, stage name, role, department, verified PayPal Sandbox receiver email), `creditCues` (cue ID, person ID, Appears in Cut v1, Appears in Cut v2, scene timestamp, cue description, reconciliation status), and `crewSettlements` (settlement ID, person ID, contract amount in USD, cut status `CREDITED` vs `GHOST_CUT` vs `ALIAS_AMBIGUOUS`, PayPal invoice/order/payout item ID, and read-back transaction status), linked via `personId` many-to-one relationships inside the AG Studio data source definition `[inferred]`
- **Two Native AG Studio Custom Widgets with Cross-Filtering**: Register two custom React widgets into the AG Studio provider using the studio widget definition contract (`formatShape` and `ai` metadata included so built-in studio agents can inspect and configure them):
  1. **Interactive Credit Roll Stage Widget**: Renders a playable, rewindable, keyboard-accessible cinema end-credits roll with a v1/v2 cut toggle, per-person status badges (`PAID`, `PENDING`, `UNPAID_GHOST`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `FAILED`), and a Behind-the-Scenes Supporter Pack PayPal checkout trigger; clicking any contributor chip invokes studio cross-filtering by `personId` `[evidence: PayPal-AI-Hackathon-v2-Cross-Domain-Ideas.zh-TW.md#Credit-Roll-Live]`
  2. **Ghost Diff & Identity Patchbay Widget**: Displays the side-by-side v1-to-v2 cut diff, stage-name-to-legal-contract evidence links, deterministic guardrail checklist, and human-in-the-loop approval controls for triggering PayPal Sandbox Invoicing QR generation, Orders capture, and Batch Payouts settlement `[evidence: paypal-v2-ideas-sol.md#Credit-Current]`
- **Deterministic Financial Guardrail Engine**: Implement a pure, side-effect-free reconciliation and policy validation module that computes all monetary totals in integer cents strictly from the locked contract table, discards any monetary number proposed by an LLM, blocks payout eligibility for rows in `NEEDS_REVIEW` or missing a whitelisted Sandbox receiver email, preserves obligation for `GHOST_CUT` rows whose contract status is approved, and generates a deterministic `PayPal-Request-Id` and `sender_batch_id` from the canonical proposal version hash so concurrent or repeated clicks return the existing batch record instead of issuing duplicate transfers `[evidence: PayPal-AI-Hackathon-v2-Cross-Domain-Ideas.zh-TW.md#Credit-Roll-Live]`
- **Server-Only PayPal Sandbox Write & Read-Back Client**: Keep PayPal credentials strictly on the server and add dedicated write and read-back functions covering:
  1. **Orders v2 (`POST /v2/checkout/orders` with `intent: CAPTURE`, `POST /v2/checkout/orders/{id}/capture`, and `GET /v2/checkout/orders/{id}`)**: For instant Behind-the-Scenes Supporter Pack funding and autonomous Sandbox verification `[evidence: paypal-v2-ideas-astra.md#Afterglow]`
  2. **Invoicing v2 (`POST /v2/invoicing/invoices`, `POST /v2/invoicing/invoices/{id}/send`, `POST /v2/invoicing/invoices/{id}/generate-qr-code`, and `GET /v2/invoicing/invoices/{id}`)**: For generating itemized production invoices and live scannable QR codes in Sandbox `[evidence: paypal-v2-ideas-grok.md#GrooveCut]`
  3. **Batch Payouts v1 (`POST /v1/payments/payouts`, `GET /v1/payments/payouts/{batch_id}`, and `GET /v1/payments/payouts-item/{item_id}`)**: For multi-recipient crew settlement with per-item status polling (`SUCCESS`, `PENDING`, `UNCLAIMED`, `FAILED`) and non-retry on POST mutations without an explicit idempotency key `[evidence: paypal-ref-payouts.md]`
- **Studio Agent Collaboration Wiring**: Configure the studio AI harness with domain-specific prompt starters and reconciliation context so the assistant can identify ghosted crew members, explain stage-name evidence, and coordinate with built-in data and widget agents while enforcing that all PayPal write endpoints require an explicit user-signed approval token from the Patchbay widget `[evidence: PayPal-AI-Hackathon-v2-Cross-Domain-Ideas.zh-TW.md#1-PayPal-AI-Hackathon]`

## Testing Decisions

- **What Makes a Good Test**: Tests must verify observable external behavior at the two confirmed seams—given v1/v2 credit lists, cue sheets, and contract rosters, assert the exact reconciliation states, guardrail blocks, idempotency keys, PayPal Sandbox API payloads/read-back state transitions, and AG Studio relational data/cross-filter outputs without coupling to internal private helpers.
- **Seam 1 — CreditRoll Reconcile, Guardrail & PayPal Settlement Seam**: Unit and integration tests covering (a) v1 vs v2 Ghost Credit detection (`GHOST_CUT` retains full approved contract payout), (b) stage-name resolution (`DJ Owl` → verified contract vs ambiguous alias → `NEEDS_REVIEW` blocked from payout), (c) integer-cent sum enforcement and LLM price-override rejection, (d) idempotent `sender_batch_id` / `PayPal-Request-Id` deduplication on rapid double-submit, and (e) live PayPal Sandbox smoke verification of Invoicing draft/QR, Orders create, and Payouts/read-back handling.
- **Seam 2 — Studio Data & Custom Widget Cross-Filter Seam**: Tests verifying that `buildStudioData()` exposes the crew, cue, and settlement tables with valid `many-to-one` relationships, and that interacting with a contributor node or ghost credit in the Custom Widgets dispatches the expected cross-filter payload (`personId`) and renders distinct badges for `SUCCESS`, `PENDING`, `UNCLAIMED`, `FAILED`, and `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- **Prior Art**: Follow the existing strict TypeScript and Next.js server/client boundary conventions in the repository, adding a dedicated test runner script (`node --test` with TypeScript loader or Vitest) plus Playwright E2E verification of the rendered AG Studio dashboard.

## Out of Scope

- Full-length video audio fingerprinting or automatic optical character recognition (OCR) on raw MP4 video frames (v1 and v2 credit lists and cue sheets are provided as structured text/manifest inputs).
- Automatic tax withholding (1099/W-8BEN calculation), multi-currency FX conversion, or legal copyright ownership arbitration.
- Unattended AI-triggered payouts without human approval in the widget UI.
